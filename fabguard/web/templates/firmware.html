Firmware Staging

Firmware updates are currently unsanctioned and undocumented. This records the image, stages it with a read-back hash check, and logs the operator-declared application alongside the version the machine actually reports.

The service does not flash the printer. Applying firmware on an MK4 is a physical act at the panel, and a service that could remotely re-flash every machine on the segment would be a far more attractive target than one that cannot. So "applied" is a claim by an operator plus an observation of what the machine reports afterwards. A disagreement between the two is a finding.

Record an image


Verification is against the image you recorded, not against a vendor signature — Prusa's .bbf signing is not a published interface this prototype can check. Integrity, not authenticity.
