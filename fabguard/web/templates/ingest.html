Ingest Mediation

Technical data entering the governed workflow is quarantined, checked, hashed and recorded in the manifest with the origin you declare. Nothing reaches the repository or a machine without passing through here.

Present an artifact


The declared origin is your claim. The service records who said it and when; it cannot verify it. That is what makes this attribution rather than authentication — and why the claim is worth having.

What is checked

  • 3MF post-process scan (CVE-2023-47268). PrusaSlicer ≤ 2.6.1 executes a post_process directive embedded in a 3MF during slicing. A 3MF from a public model repository is therefore command execution against the workstation that opens it. Any 3MF carrying one is refused.
  • Container sanity. A 3MF must open as a zip, and no member path may escape the archive root.
  • Extension allow-list and a size cap.

Three specific checks, not general format normalisation. A refused artifact stays in quarantine and the refusal is recorded.
