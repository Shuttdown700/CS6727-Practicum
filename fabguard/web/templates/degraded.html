Degraded-Mode Operation

When the mediated path is unavailable, fabrication continues under a logged, time-limited, reported exception rather than halting. Availability is treated as a security property here: a control that stops mission-essential work gets bypassed, and a bypass nobody designed is a bypass nobody records.

How this is meant to work

Prospective is the normal case: you see trouble coming and open the window while the portal still works.

Retrospective exists because "the service is down" and "the service records an exception" cannot both be fully true at once. A window opened after the fact is marked as such, because it is weaker evidence than one opened at the time and the log should not blur the two.

Time-limited is not decoration. An exception that never expires is a disabled control with a nicer name. Overdue windows raise a critical finding on their own.

It accounts, it does not authorize. Jobs run during a window stay unauthorized and get tagged to the exception, so the audit record is continuous across the gap rather than silent.
