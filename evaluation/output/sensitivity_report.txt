  ORDERING STABLE. No pair of variants reverses order at any shift tested,
  so the ranking survives a systematic one- and two-band estimation error in
  either direction. The claim to put in the report, with exactly this scope:

      Shifting every estimated band one or two steps in either direction
      leaves the relative ordering of the variants unchanged, so the
      comparative conclusions are robust to systematic band-assignment
      error of that magnitude.

  Ties (two variants on the same total -- a loss of resolution from band
  clamping, not a reversal):
    -2: Baseline = Observational = 57

  Bands clamp at the ends of the ladder, so totals compress sharply at +/-2 as
  leaves accumulate on Very High or Very Low. Treat the +/-1 rows as the
  informative ones and +/-2 as a stress case; the -2 column in particular
  pushes most leaves to the bottom of the scale and should not be quoted as a
  risk estimate.

  Note the observational reduction is NOT monotone in the shift (it peaks near
  the as-scored assignment). That is the coarseness of Table I-2 at work rather
  than a modelling error: a partial control only changes the score where the
  likelihood drop crosses a matrix boundary, and shifting the impact bands
  moves which leaves sit on such a boundary.

D. WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The band ASSIGNMENTS' direction. Perturbing symmetrically around each
    estimate assumes the estimates are unbiased. A systematic optimism about
    the architecture's own leaves would not show up here.
  - The lookup matrices. Tables G-5 and I-2 are held fixed; their coarseness
    at high impact is a structural property of the method, not sampling error.
  - The tree structure. Leaf membership and the control mapping are fixed.
  - Independence. Monte Carlo draws each band independently; real estimation
    error is likely correlated within a tree, which would widen the interval.
    The deterministic sweep in section A is the fully-correlated bound and
    brackets that case.
